One email, not four

Everyone on the To line is being asked to do something. Everyone on CC is being kept informed. They arrive as one email with both lines filled, so a reply reaches the thread and everybody can see who else knows — which is the thing four separate emails destroy. Nobody appears twice, and nobody who is on To is also on CC.

Blank means the built-in list is still in force. Every change bumps a version number, and that version is recorded on each email, so an email sent in November can still be explained in March.
