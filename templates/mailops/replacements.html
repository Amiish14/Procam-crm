Why this is a table

When a lead would be assigned to somebody who has left, it goes to their replacement instead. The list changes whenever somebody leaves, and a deploy is the wrong unit for that — so it lives here rather than in code.

Chains are followed: if A was replaced by B and B has since left, B's row applies. A replacement who is not active is refused, because it would only move the problem.
