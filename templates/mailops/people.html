Why this screen exists

Most undeliverable CRM email was never a mail problem. It was an address on the employee record that could not work: a domain the CRM is not allowed to write to, a placeholder minted from the employee code so a form would save, or the same mailbox on two people — where whoever reads the notification first dismisses it for the other.

None of these appear on the Email Health screen, because a message the CRM never sent leaves no row. Allowed domains: {% for d in domains %}@{{ d }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}.
