Duplicate leads

One enquiry entered more than once. Pairs are found on four signals and each is worth something different: a shared GSTIN is one legal entity exactly, a shared email domain can be two — so what matched is shown rather than a score on its own.

Nothing is deleted. The lead you fold away keeps its number and its history, is archived with "merged into #N", and the merge can be put back — every activity, mail and quote that moved is recorded by row id, so reversing returns exactly those and leaves the survivor's own records where they are.

This is not the account screen. One customer written twice is duplicate accounts; this is one job entered twice.
