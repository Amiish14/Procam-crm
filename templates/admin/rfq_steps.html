When a reason is asked

  • A step closed inside {{ early }}% of its standard time is Early, and asks what helped.
  • A step closed past its standard time is Delayed, and asks why before it will close.
  • Everything in between is On time and asks nothing — a step that always asks "why" trains people to pick the first option.
  • The standard the workload table measures against is {{ target }}% on time.
