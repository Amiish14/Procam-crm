Leads on no account

An open lead with no customer account, or one filed under a placeholder name. Until it has an account it cannot be quoted, and no account-level view — the history, the credit position, the 24-month record — can see it.

The proposal is the same matcher the intake uses: a learned sender, a contact on file, the account's own email or website domain, a GSTIN, or a company name that normalises exactly to an account's. certain means a domain, a GSTIN or a sender somebody already mapped. likely means the name matched exactly and nothing disagreed. possible means choose.

Reject changes nothing about the lead. It records that this account is not the customer, so the same wrong suggestion stops arriving every morning. A lead that is not an enquiry at all — a circular, a vessel list — is Informational — not a lead on the lead itself.

Hundreds at a time are better done on paper: the same proposals come out as one CSV per vertical from scripts/2026_11_11_link_accounts.py --check --csv, and only ticked rows are applied.
