# User Guide

For sales, pre-sales and operations staff using the Procam CRM at
`https://procamlogitech.com/CRM`.

## Signing in

- Sign in with your employee code and password.
- A new or reset account starts with a **temporary password** from your
  administrator. It works for 72 hours, and you must choose your own
  before anything else opens: at least 10 characters, not containing
  your employee code, and not a common password.
- After 8 wrong passwords your account locks for 15 minutes. If you have
  forgotten your password, ask a CRM Administrator to reset it.
- Your session ends after 8 hours, when your password is reset, or when
  you choose **Sign out everywhere** (use it if you signed in on a shared
  or lost device).

## What you can see

You see the leads, accounts, contacts and deals your access allows —
usually your own, your vertical's if you head one, or everything if you
administer the CRM. If a link opens "not found", the record belongs to
someone outside your access; ask its owner or your manager.

On someone else's account page you see who owns it. If you are working a
lead there, you also see your own leads, opportunities and the account's
contacts.

## Your day

- **My Work** — open leads, follow-ups due today and overdue, leads with
  no contact for a while.
- **Sales Pipeline** — leads by stage; drag or edit to move them on.
- **Lead screen** — details, owners, the email trail with the customer,
  rate-sourcing emails, notes, history.

## Leads

- **Why this is a lead**: the lead screen shows what the intake decided
  about the email that created it (its class, confidence and signals) and
  how sure it was of the vertical. If it is wrong, tell your
  administrator — corrections teach the classifier.
- **Notes**: add a call note or meeting summary. You can edit your own
  notes; the earlier wording is kept, and **History** shows every version
  with the changes highlighted. Notes never touch the customer's email.
- **Reassigning**: owners are changed by administrators, with a reason
  that is kept in the history.
- **Email trail**: the original enquiry is never overwritten.
- **Value**: the lead's **Opportunity value** — your best estimate of the
  deal, in rupees (or USD/EUR), with how firm it is. Lists show it in
  lakh (₹ L), or crore once it reaches ₹1 crore.
- **Quote**: when a lead reaches RFQ Generated or Quoted, fill the Quote
  block — quote number, the value quoted, the date it went out, how long
  it is valid (a date, or a number of days), and optionally the cost, for
  the margin. Changing a quote later keeps the earlier version under
  **Earlier versions**. Once a quote's validity passes while the lead is
  still Quoted or Under Negotiation, the lead shows **quote lapsed** —
  follow up.
- If the CRM found a quote in one of your quotation emails, it offers it
  on the lead (**Use these**); nothing counts until you save it.

## Accounts

- **Open** an account for its activity timeline, PIC history and stage
  history, and to log an activity, change its stage or convert it to an
  opportunity.
- **Reassign PIC** (if your access allows it): choose the primary PIC,
  optionally a secondary PIC who monitors or backs up the account, and
  give a reason. The change and the reason are kept in the account's PIC
  history.
- **Many accounts at once**: tick the accounts in the list — the box in
  the header ticks every account the current search and filters show,
  never hidden ones — then **Assign N accounts to PIC**. Leave the
  secondary PIC on "Keep each account's current secondary PIC" to change
  only the primary. When it finishes you see how many succeeded; any
  that failed stay ticked, with the reason, so you can retry them.

## Notes Search

Sidebar → **Notes Search**. Search the notes on leads you can see, and
filter by author and dates. Recent notes and notes with more of your
words rank first.

## Procam AI (Copilot)

Ask in plain words: "my open leads", "follow-ups due this week", "RFQs
waiting for a quote", "search the emails for crane availability".

- Answers come from the CRM, within your access — the Copilot cannot
  show you anything your screens would not.
- Rows link to the records. **From the CRM** marks facts; **Suggestion**
  marks advice.
- If a question is ambiguous it asks which you meant.
- Mark an answer **Not helpful** and say what was wrong; it is reviewed.

## Deals

- **RFQs**: raise an RFQ from a lead; rate lines go to the sourcing desk.
- **Quotes**: prepare, submit for approval, send; a quote is approved by
  a vertical head or administrator, never by the person who prepared it.
- **Won**: capture the customer PO on the handover before operations can
  create the project.

## Business cards

Photograph a card; check the fields read from it; save it as a new
contact or link it to an existing one. Only you (and administrators)
see the cards you scan.

## Good habits

- Log every call or meeting as a note or activity the same day.
- Keep follow-up dates and expected close dates real.
- Close leads and opportunities you have lost, with a reason.
- Never paste passwords or customer bank details into notes.
